### 📌 Cultural & DJ Guidance for this ceremony:
- **Kahe Par Rachahu Mehndiya A Sakhi**: The soothing signature Bagheli henna folk song asking about love and beauty.
- **Haathan Mehndi Chamke Banna Tore Naam Ki**: Sung by mothers and grandmothers during intricate bridal/family henna application.
- **Sasural Mein Bhabhi Tore Raj Ho**: Affectionate teasing welcoming the new sister-in-law into the family dynasty.
- **Banna Bada Albela (Dholak Geet)**: Playful, dignified banter about the groom's handsome poise and charm.
- **Mhare Aanganwa Chhan Chhan Baaje**: Folk dholak rhythm describing tinkling anklets (painjaniya) in the ancestral home.
- **Mehndi Rachi Vindhya Nagari**: Vindhya folk-pop hit ideal for sister/bhabhi sangeet stage dance routine.
- **Banno Rani Chamke Ri (Bagheli Pop)**: Fast tempo festive beat with modern keyboard and Bagheli acoustic dholak.

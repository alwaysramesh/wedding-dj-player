### 📌 Cultural & DJ Guidance for this ceremony:
- **Hardi Chadhawa Mor Bhaiya Ke**: Elder aunts and bhabhis applying fragrant haldi and mustard oil ubtan.
- **Piyari Range Mor Dulha Ke Chola**: Celebrates the auspicious yellow robe and turmeric glow of the groom.
- **Nahchhu Gaavahu Sakhiyaan Mil Ke**: Auspicious folk ritual of nail paring and ceremonial bath before donning wedding silks.
- **Chauka Purao Dhani Maai**: Drawing flour and turmeric alpana patterns on the floor for the groom's seat.
- **Hari-Hari Dubhi Chadhai**: Grass tips dipped in oil and milk touched to the groom's feet, knees, and shoulders.
- **Hardi Lagau Sajna Ke (Bagheli Mix)**: Lively Bagheli beat that gets cousins and bhabhis dancing while applying haldi.
- **Banna Ke Ang Ang Hardi Sajai**: High energy modern Vindhyan beat perfect for the marigold flower shower.

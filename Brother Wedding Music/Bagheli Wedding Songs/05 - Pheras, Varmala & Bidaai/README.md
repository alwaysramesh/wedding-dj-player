### 📌 Cultural & DJ Guidance for this ceremony:
- **Varmala Pehirao Mor Banna**: Garland exchange moment highlighting traditional reverence and joy.
- **Sindoor Daan Karahu Mor Raja**: Deeply emotional and sacred moment when the groom fills the bride's maang.
- **Saat Phere Lehu Banna-Banni**: Gentle acoustic accompaniment honoring the seven sacred vows around the holy fire.
- **Babul Ka Ghar Chhode Dulhaniya (Bidaai)**: Touching separation song sung as the bride bids tearful farewell to childhood home.
- **Kahe Ko Byahe Bides (Bagheli Folk)**: Classic Amir Khusro folk adaptation deeply rooted in Vindhya folk singing traditions.
- **Sajani Bida Hoti Jaai (Emotional)**: Gentle flute and sarangi fusion ballad capturing the dignity and love of parents.
- **Banna Banni Ke Shubh Vivah**: Joyful blessing anthem played as the newlyweds take blessings from elders.

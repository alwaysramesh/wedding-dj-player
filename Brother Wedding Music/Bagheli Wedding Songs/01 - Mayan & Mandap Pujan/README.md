### 📌 Cultural & DJ Guidance for this ceremony:
- **Avadhpuri Mora Dhaam (Janakpur Byahan Jaiyo)**: Traditional Bagheli Banra comparing the groom to Lord Ram leaving Ayodhya to marry Sita in Janakpur.
- **Aaju Janakpur Marwa Suhaawan Lage**: Sung when the sacred bamboo and mango leaves mandap is erected in the courtyard.
- **Gauri Ke Lala Shubh Din Aaye**: Invoking Lord Ganesha and Kuldevi for an obstacle-free (Nirvighna) wedding.
- **Bhaiya Ka Tilak Chadhawa Aaye**: Paternal relatives applying Chandan and Akshat on the groom's forehead.
- **Sat Seek Ke Pinjadwa (Bagheli Banra)**: One of the oldest Bagheli folk meters describing the graceful home and upbringing of the groom.
- **Mangal Baaje Baghelkhand Mein**: Modern acoustic production with Shehnai and Dholak, ideal for family morning tea.
- **Tilak Chadhau Mor Banna**: Trending across Vindhyan Instagram reels and weddings in Rewa and Satna.

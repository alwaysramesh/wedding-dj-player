### 📌 Cultural & DJ Guidance for this ceremony:
- **Ghodi Chadhe Mor Veer Kunwar**: Sister feeding grains to the mare and holding the reins as the brother mounts.
- **Sehra Sajao Mor Banna Ke Maathe**: Golden sehra tied with blessings from mother, father, chacha, and mama.
- **Banna Chale Sasural Byahan**: Procession moving forward amidst brass bands and dholak beats.
- **Dwar Pe Aaye Mor Dulhe Raja (Agwani)**: Mother-in-law performing traditional aarti and welcoming the groom at the venue gate.
- **Rewa Ka Banna Aaye Re (DJ Mix)**: The #1 trending Bagheli DJ truck track across Rewa, Satna, and Sidhi baraats.
- **Dulha Bane Mor Veer (Baraat Blast)**: Dhol-tasha fusion track for cousins and friends dancing ahead of the groom's car/chariot.

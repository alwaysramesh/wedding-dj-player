### 📑 E. Queue Management
- Click **"📑 Queue"** to slide out the upcoming playlist panel.
- Click **"➕"** on any song to add it to the end of the queue.
- Click **"⏭"** to set a song as "Up Next" (plays immediately after the current song).
- Reorder or remove songs from the queue at any time.
- **"Clear Queue"** resets the playlist with one click.

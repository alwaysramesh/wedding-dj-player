## 🎯 3. Critical Musical Cues (Zero Error Policy)

### Cue 1: The Bride's Entry (Walkway to Stage)
- **Track Option:** `Din Shagna Da` (Jasleen Royal) OR `Kudmayi (Bridal Walk Cut)` OR `Madhanya`
- **Execution:** Dim ambient venue lighting. Spotlights on the Bride under the Phoolon Ki Chaadar. Volume at 80 dB.

### Cue 2: The Varmala Garland Exchange Moment (Cold Pyros Drop)
- **Track:** `Kudmayi` (Arijit Singh / Shreya Ghoshal) OR `Mangalyam Tantunanena` (A.R. Rahman)
- **Execution:** When the couple raises the garlands, bring track volume up. Hit the musical crescendo **at the exact second** the cold pyros ignite and floral shower begins.

### Cue 3: During the Seven Sacred Pheras
- **Track Selection:** `Raag Yaman (Sitar & Bansuri)` / `Mangal Parinay (Shehnai)` - Ustad Bismillah Khan
- **Execution:** **Strictly instrumental and low ambient (50 dB)**. Pundit ji's microphone must be loud, warm, and crystal clear without any speaker feedback.

### Cue 4: The Vidaai (Farewell Embrace)
- **Track Selection:** `Dilbaro` (Raazi) transitioning into `Babul Ki Duayen Leti Ja` (Mohammed Rafi)
- **Execution:** Respectful, heart-touching volume (65 dB). Do not play upbeat party tracks until the doli car has fully departed.

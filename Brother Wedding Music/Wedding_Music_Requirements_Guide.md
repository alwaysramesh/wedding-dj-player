## 🎧 DJ Audio & Sound Engineering Checklist

1. **Morning Functions (Mayan, Haldi, Rituals):**
   - Sound Profile: Clear mid-range and highs, acoustic warmth, low punchy sub-bass.
   - Volume: 60-70 dB so elderly relatives and priests can communicate comfortably.
   - Recommended setup: 2x 15" Active Tops with wireless lapel microphones for pandit ji.

2. **Evening Sangeet & DJ Night:**
   - Sound Profile: Deep sub-bass (30Hz–80Hz punch), crisp high frequencies for Punjabi dhol kicks.
   - Volume: 90-105 dB on the dance floor with directional monitors.
   - Equipment: Line array system + dual 18" subwoofers, smoke machines, cold pyros for drop moments.

3. **Baraat & Groom Entry:**
   - Mobile sound truck / Dhol party coordination: Keep sync between live live Dholis and DJ truck.
   - Transition Cue: When Baraat arrives at the venue gate, crossfade from Dhol to "Malhari" or "Tauba Tauba" drop.

4. **Varmala & Pheras:**
   - Zero-latency wireless mic on stage for the bride & groom exchange.
   - Instrumental volume drop right when vows or mantra recitations begin.
